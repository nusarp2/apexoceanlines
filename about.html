About Us

A freight forwarder built for how shipping works today

Apex Ocean Lines is a newly launched forwarder. We started it because too much of freight forwarding still runs on phone tag, PDFs and guesswork — and we think shippers deserve better.

Our Approach

Fewer hand-offs, more accountability

Most freight forwarders route your shipment through whichever team is free that day. We do the opposite: one specialist owns your account from quote to delivery, so you're never re-explaining your shipment to someone new.

We're not the biggest name in the industry yet — we're a new company built by people who've worked inside freight forwarding and logistics operations and got tired of watching avoidable delays happen. Our network of ocean, air and customs partners is built to move real cargo from day one, not to look good on a slide.

What Guides Us

The standards we hold every shipment to

Transparency

Itemized quotes and honest timelines — including when something is genuinely uncertain, instead of a number that just sounds good.

Responsiveness

A shipment issue at 11pm shouldn't wait until 9am the next day to get a reply. We treat response time as a core part of the service.

Getting the Details Right

Most delays trace back to a documentation error made weeks earlier. We check the paperwork before it becomes a problem at the port.

Want to be one of our first clients?

We're building our client base one shipment at a time — and giving early clients the direct attention that gets harder to offer at scale.
