Shipment Tracking

Always know where your cargo stands

Every shipment gets a named specialist and a clear update rhythm — so you find out about a delay from us, not from a missed delivery date.

How It Works

From booking confirmation to proof of delivery

1

Booking confirmed

You receive a written booking confirmation with carrier, routing and estimated transit time.

2

Departure notice

We confirm once your cargo has departed, along with the vessel/flight details and expected arrival window.

3

In-transit updates

Your account specialist flags any schedule changes, transshipments or customs holds as soon as we know about them — not on a fixed weekly check-in.

4

Arrival & customs clearance

We manage clearance at the port of entry and let you know the moment your cargo is released.

5

Final delivery

Delivery confirmation and documentation are sent once your cargo reaches its final destination.
